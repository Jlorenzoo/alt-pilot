Bedrock Account Setup

How to Set Up Bedrock

Already have a Microsoft Java account?

Every Microsoft account that owns Minecraft Java Edition already owns Bedrock Edition for PC at no extra cost (it is included in the official Minecraft bundle).

⚠️ Important requirement: You must launch Minecraft Bedrock at least once on this Microsoft account (via the Minecraft Launcher on PC, console, or mobile) before ALT Pilot can authenticate. Opening Bedrock initializes your Xbox player identity and Bedrock multiplayer license with Mojang. Without this one-time launch, sign-in will fail with UNAUTHORIZED.

Setting up a separate Bedrock alt?

  1. Create a free Microsoft account with birth year 18+ (required for online multiplayer without parental locks).
  2. Claim your Xbox Gamertag on xbox.com (or in the Minecraft Launcher).
  3. Launch Minecraft Bedrock at least once with that account to activate the profile.

Connecting in ALT Pilot

Choose "Java + Bedrock" to link both identities from one Microsoft account (Java stays active, and Bedrock is added as a companion alt). Or choose "Bedrock Edition" for a standalone alt. Bedrock alts join Floodgate servers like Complex Skyblock with a dot prefix (e.g. .Username).
